# ORBIT topic research

YouTube public video research is primary; Google Trending Now RSS is a separate supporting signal. Reddit and TikTok research APIs are not silently enabled. At most three YouTube search requests per research task; detail/channel requests are batched. Public view, like and comment counts remain labelled YouTube facts. Short duration alone does not prove a Shorts classification, retention, active audience interest or a causal hook advantage.

The comparative module uses same-channel, similar-duration and similar-age observations; at least ten distinct comparable videos and a nontrivial baseline are required. It never reconstructs historical counts from current lifetime counts. Custom ratios remain disabled unless actual platform analytics approval is recorded in integrations/youtube/analytics-policy.json with approved=true and an approval_reference. Human interest in the feature is not platform approval. Do not create a fictional approval record. No combined Google/YouTube opportunity score is presented. Raw observations expire after 30 days.

Research produces exact source URLs and a labelled handoff. Explicit news still requires dated articles and matching fresh footage. A fresh YouTube topic candidate is not automatically a news event; it has its own labelled research evidence and is rechecked for freshness before download. Media source access and reuse rights are distinct.

Main assistant operation: reuse the existing team; never create duplicate agents for a new video. Natural subject/creator requests can use the typed production controller. A subject clarification may complete the immediately preceding blocked video brief and must preserve its duration. Do not start from arbitrary fragments, examples, negation or quoted error reports. A saved preference is not an implemented code fix. Unfilled model templates must not be accepted as completed answers. Existing local-only/model/privacy settings still govern model requests.
